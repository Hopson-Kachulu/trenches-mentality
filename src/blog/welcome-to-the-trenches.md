Everybody talks about "making it". Very few people talk about the middle part: the long stretch where you're learning, failing, trying again and wondering if any of it is working.

That middle part is the trenches. And that's where most of us actually live.

## Why this exists

I started Trenches Mentality because I wanted a place where people share the real version. The tools that actually helped. The money that was lost. The farm season that went wrong and what was learned. The first client, the first sale, the first time something finally clicked.

## What you'll find here

Every story here falls under one of five ways out:

- **Tech**: building, fixing and selling with tools anyone can get.
- **AI**: using AI as a co-worker, and the work that actually pays.
- **Agriculture**: soil, seeds, livestock and the business of feeding people.
- **Arts**: music, design, film and words, turned into a living.
- **Hustle**: money, discipline, side income and the mindset that holds it together.

## How you can be part of it

Join the Sunday letter. Send us your story through the contact page. And when you climb a step, reach back for the person behind you.

That's the whole idea. See you in the next one.
